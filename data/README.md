# Dataset

This project uses ERA5 reanalysis data from the Copernicus Climate Data Store.

The raw ERA5 dataset will not be stored directly in this repository because of its large size.

## Planned Variables

- 2 m temperature
- 2 m dewpoint temperature
- 10 m u-component of wind
- 10 m v-component of wind
- Mean sea-level pressure
- Surface pressure
- Total precipitation

## Data Source

Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS)

ERA5 will be downloaded as a regional subset for the experiments.
