# Literature Review

This folder contains notes and analysis of the research papers
selected for the project.

## Research Focus

The literature review focuses on:

- Weather and climate foundation models
- AI-based weather forecasting
- Earth system foundation models
- Hyperlocal forecasting
- Model evaluation and generalization
- Local adaptation and downscaling
- Climate intelligence and decision support

## Selected Models

The literature review will investigate models including:

- FourCastNet
- GraphCast
- Pangu-Weather
- NeuralGCM
- Aurora
- ClimaX
- Earth observation foundation models

## Paper Selection

The project will review 10 recent research papers published
in 2025/2026 from IEEE, Elsevier, and Springer journals,
as required for the project review.
